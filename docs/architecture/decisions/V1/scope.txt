# Workflow

## Quote creation
Establish customer, dates and some additional paramaters.
Without pricing, that is for V2, including pricing engine.

## Product specifications
Create a product with defined dimensions and specifications.

## Quote submission and storage
Submit the quote to be send to AWS, and stored, no pricing engine required at this point.

## AWS response
Receive a response from AWS.
